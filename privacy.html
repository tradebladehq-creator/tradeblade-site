Privacy Policy

TradeBlade Technologies LLC · Effective October 3, 2026

This policy explains what data TradeBlade collects, why, where it goes, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. We have tried to write it the way we would want it explained to us: specifically, and without padding. The software shows you the same information on a screen before it opens for the first time, and nothing is sent until you accept it there.

1. What we collect — website

  • Sign-up form: your email address, your broker name (optional), and your language preference. That's all the form sends.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no ad trackers. This website does not set cookies, does not run analytics scripts, and does not embed advertising or social trackers.

2. What the software sends

TradeBlade reports how the program is running and how it is being used to TradeBlade Technologies LLC. We use it to keep the program healthy and to learn how TradeBlade is used. This is part of what you get for the price: there is no switch to turn it off. This is what is sent:

  • Your trades made with TradeBlade — the instrument, direction, number of contracts, entry, stop and target prices, how long the trade lasted, why it ended, and its result in dollars.
  • While TradeBlade is running, its current state every few seconds — open positions and the session profit or loss on each account TradeBlade is using, and market prices for the instruments you trade.
  • The type of each account, not its number — evaluation, funded, live or sim.
  • The balance of your prop-firm accounts — evaluation and funded accounts only — and any payout taken from them.
  • Your TradeBlade settings — contracts, hedge size, entry mode, loss limits and trading window.
  • How you use the program — which buttons and features you use, and warnings the program shows.
  • Program health — TradeBlade, Windows and NinjaTrader versions, memory use, connection status, warnings, errors and crash reports.
  • Your licence key and a machine ID — the machine ID is a code made from your computer's hardware. It is not your computer's name.

All of this is linked to your licence, and so to the email address you signed up with.

3. What is never sent

  • Your account numbers. On your PC, each account number is replaced by a code that cannot be turned back into the number — not by us, not by anyone. The same account always gets the same code, so we can tell it is the same account without ever knowing its number.
  • Your name, your Windows user name, or your computer's name.
  • The balance of your own brokerage account. How much money is in your live account is never sent — only the trades TradeBlade makes there.
  • Your broker or prop-firm logins and passwords. TradeBlade never sees them.
  • Anything on your computer that is not TradeBlade or NinjaTrader's own log files.

We have no access to your funds. TradeBlade sends trade instructions through NinjaTrader only; deposits, withdrawals, and custody stay entirely with your broker. Payments for TradeBlade are processed by Stripe; card data goes to Stripe, not to us.

4. Log files — only when you say yes

If you ask us for help, we may request TradeBlade and NinjaTrader log files. TradeBlade shows you which files and what time period, and sends them only if you press Allow for that request. Log files are sent as they are, so they can contain your account numbers and your Windows user name.

5. What we do with it, and what we don't

  • We use it to issue and check licences, to find and fix problems, and to understand how TradeBlade is used and how its traders trade.
  • We never sell it and never share it. Not with advertisers, not with anyone. If TradeBlade is ever sold or merged, your data would pass to the new owner under these same terms.
  • We never use it to trade, to copy your trades, or to trade against you.
  • Only TradeBlade Technologies LLC and people working for it who need the data to support the product can see it.
  • When TradeBlade connects, our servers see your internet address (IP), as any internet service does. It is not saved with your data.
  • We use your sign-up email to send the materials you requested and updates about TradeBlade. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.

6. Where it goes (processors)

  • Cloudflare — hosts our services and stores licence, telemetry, and log data on its global network. Data may be processed outside your country.
  • Kit (email platform) — stores the sign-up list and sends our emails.
  • Stripe — payment processing.

7. How long we keep it

  • The current-state report: only the latest one is kept; each new one replaces the last.
  • Trade, usage and problem records, crash reports and any log files you allowed: 90 days.
  • A daily summary (counts and totals per day), a running total and last balance per prop-firm account, and payouts: 2 years.
  • Sign-up list: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete you.
  • Licence records: for the life of the licence plus any legally required period.

8. The phone app

If you turn on the phone app, what your PC and phone send each other is encrypted from end to end. We cannot read it.

9. Your choices and your rights

  • Delete my data: email support@tradebladehq.com from your licence email and we will delete everything we hold for your licence.
  • Object: email the same address and we will switch your licence to send program health and crash reports only — no trades, results, balances or usage.
  • Decline: if you do not agree, press Exit on the screen TradeBlade shows before it first opens. TradeBlade will not open and nothing is sent.

Email us from the address attached to your licence or sign-up and we will, within 30 days: tell you what we hold about you, export it to you, correct it, or delete it. If you are in the EU/UK, these correspond to your GDPR rights of access, portability, rectification, erasure and objection; you may also lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. If you are a California resident, note that we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

10. How it is protected

It is sent over an encrypted connection, and each report is signed with your licence. It is stored encrypted by Cloudflare. Seeing it requires a separate login and key. When you ask us to delete your data, copies can remain in our host's automatic backups for up to 30 days before they are gone. No system is perfectly secure, but the attack surface here is deliberately small.

11. Age

TradeBlade is for adults. You must be 18 or older to use the software.

12. Changes

If what the software sends changes, TradeBlade shows you the new text and asks you to accept it again before it sends anything under the new terms. We also post the new version here with a new effective date.
